## The Story

Every brand I worked with was losing the same conversations. Someone would post on Reddit asking "what's the best tool for X?" - a perfect fit for the brand - and by the time anyone on the team saw it, the thread had ninety replies and the moment was gone. A news article would mention their competitor. A local business would tag them on Twitter with a complaint. All of it happening in real time. None of it reaching them.

The enterprise social-listening tools that promise to fix this - Brand24, Mention, Sprout - run three to five hundred a month minimum. And most of them are optimized for reporting, not response. By the time the weekly report lands, the conversation is a week cold.

## What I Built

I built a listening layer that runs a few times a day and quietly scans the corners of the internet the brand actually cares about - target subreddits, industry news, keyword mentions. It reads every hit, decides whether it's a sales opportunity, a support signal, a PR risk, or just noise, and hands only the useful ones to the team via Slack. Every high-priority mention includes the source link, a one-line summary of what's being said, the suggested tone of response, and a nudge on who should own it.

The team stops guarding a search dashboard. Slack tells them when the internet actually mentioned them in a way that matters.

## The Outcome

Time-to-respond on real opportunities dropped from days to under an hour. The team started converting Reddit threads and news mentions into pipeline because they got there while the conversation was still live. Cost per scan is a fraction of a cent - a tiny fraction of what Brand24 costs. And the founder finally stopped feeling like the internet was talking about their brand behind their back.
