## The Story

Vanguard Records had been running their label out of a stock WordPress theme that didn't match the energy of their releases. They wanted something raw — editorial typography, dark and red-drenched, with clear paths for fans, tourgoers, and demo submitters.

## What We Built

A high-contrast label site anchored by a "JOIN THE COLLECTIVE" hero, live tour dates, a new releases wall, an artist directory, and a first-class "Submit Demo" flow. Every module is MDX-driven so their A&R team can push new artists, releases, and tour dates without touching code.

## The Outcome

Demo submissions tripled in the first month. Tour ticket click-throughs from the site climbed sharply once the "Live Dates" module went from a PDF to a live component.
