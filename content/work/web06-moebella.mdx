## The Story

MoeBella runs a premium hair transformation studio in Surrey — 100% Remy human hair, certified master stylists, tension-free protective styling. Her old site looked like every other salon site on the high street. She wanted the online experience to feel as considered as the chair experience.

## What We Built

A dark-luxe Next.js site built around a "Guaranteed Reservation" booking flow — WhatsApp-first, phone-second, so serious clients get contacted the same day. The transformations gallery is a filterable before/after wall (extensions, braiding, wigs, styling, coloring, treatments). A short AI styling quiz routes visitors to the right consultation type, and a bespoke services menu doubles as an in-person price sheet. Sanity CMS lets her push new gallery entries and services from her phone.

## The Outcome

Bookings per week roughly tripled in the first month, and the average consultation now converts at a materially higher rate because the site pre-qualifies length, service, and budget before the call.
