## The Story

Every founder I spoke with had the same quiet complaint - they were spending their sharpest hours on tasks a system could handle. Answering the same emails. Copy-pasting leads into CRMs. Chasing the same follow-ups. The work that actually mattered - building, selling, thinking - kept getting pushed to the edges of the day.

The problem wasn't a tools problem. It was a "nobody has time to stitch the tools together" problem. So I made myself a promise - thirty days, thirty workflows, one every day. Real automations built for real businesses. No demos. No toy examples. Each one solving a specific pain a real founder feels every week.

## What I Built

Day one was the founder's-choice kickoff - a public commitment to ship one automation per day for the next month. Each build starts with a real pain point, gets designed as a self-contained workflow, and ends up cheap enough to run for pennies. The point wasn't to build one big platform. It was to prove that most business bottlenecks are actually just missing wiring - and the wiring, if you understand the pattern, takes hours, not weeks.

## The Outcome

Thirty workflows shipped. Every one of them runs for under a cent per execution. Every one of them replaces a task a human used to do by hand. And the through-line - the thing this whole challenge was really testing - is that the gap between "we should automate this" and "it's live" is almost never technical. It's just someone deciding to do it.
