## The Story

ACI Canteen was running a 2,000-employee canteen off spreadsheets — daily entries, ingredient stock, supplier ledgers, per-head cost targets. Leadership couldn't see cost overruns until the month closed, and low-stock ingredients were only caught when someone physically noticed an empty bin.

## What We Built

A full executive dashboard on Next.js + Supabase with role-based admin access, live cost-per-head tracking, low-stock alerting, supplier-wise ingredient history, daily entry forms, and cost trend analytics. Everything syncs in real time — no more end-of-month surprises.

## The Outcome

Leadership now catches cost drift on the same day it happens. Low-stock warnings prevent the 3–4 emergency runs per month that used to inflate supplier costs. The system replaced four separate spreadsheets and a Telegram group.
