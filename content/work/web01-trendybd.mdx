## The Story

TrendyBD sells trending imports from China's biggest tech hubs — the kind of products that go viral on TikTok before a local store even hears about them. The founders needed a site that felt as fast and current as the products themselves.

## What We Built

A high-conversion e-commerce experience on Next.js with a bold orange-on-black identity, a sticky "next viral drop" email capture, WhatsApp-native inquiry flow for Bangladeshi buyers, and a cart pattern tuned for cash-on-delivery. Product pages surface trust signals — 48h delivery, 7-day replacement, 100% genuine, cash on delivery — right above the fold.

## The Outcome

The founders now push a new drop to their VIP list in minutes. First-week conversion sits well above their previous Shopify theme, and support load dropped once the "Order on WhatsApp" flow was live.
