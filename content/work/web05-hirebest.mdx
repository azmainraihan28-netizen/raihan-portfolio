## The Story

HireBest's founder was spending seven minutes on every resume that came through her pipeline. Multiply that by fifty CVs a week and it was two workdays of pattern-matching she wasn't paid for. She wanted an LLM to score CVs against a job description — cleanly, transparently, with an interface a recruiter would actually trust.

## What We Built

A clean light-mode SaaS on Next.js + Supabase with paste-or-upload screenings, per-CV fit scoring, verdict mixing (Fit / Maybe / Skip), score distribution charts, top-skill extraction across the batch, and a "most common gaps" panel that surfaces sourcing decisions. A live "time saved" widget shows exactly how much manual review the tool has removed.

## The Outcome

The tool paid for itself in the first screening. HireBest launched paid plans on day 28 and now runs recruiter pipelines across four verticals — engineering, sales, marketing, customer support.
