## The Story

Every solo agency owner I met was running three businesses at once and getting crushed by all of them. The first business was landing clients - writing proposals, following up, closing. The second was onboarding new clients - welcome emails, kickoff calls, gathering assets, setting expectations. The third was actually delivering the work while managing check-ins, milestones, and the ten small things that fall through the cracks.

Any one of these was a full-time job. Doing all three alone, at the same time, was why so many solo agencies stayed stuck at capacity. The moment they landed a client, the delivery consumed them and the pipeline went dry. Then they'd close the project, look up, and realize they had nothing lined up for next month.

## What I Built

I built them a full agency operating system in a single workflow with three smart branches. Send a signal that says "new lead" and it generates a personalized proposal in the agency's voice, drafts the follow-up sequence, and logs the deal. Send "onboard" and it fires the welcome package, schedules the kickoff, spins up the client's project folder, and drops the first check-in into the calendar. Send "delivery" and it runs the ongoing milestone comms - day-three progress note, day-seven update, invoice reminder, wrap-up email.

One workflow. Three stages. The whole agency's client lifecycle, from cold lead to closed project, running on the same rails.

## The Outcome

The solo agencies that adopted it stopped choosing between pipeline and delivery - both kept moving, because both were mostly automated. Client experience got measurably more consistent because every touchpoint was on-brand and on-time, every time. Cost per client across the full lifecycle is a fraction of a cent. And the capstone lesson from thirty days of building this - the whole point of the challenge - is that you don't need a team to run like one. You need the right thirty workflows in the right order.
