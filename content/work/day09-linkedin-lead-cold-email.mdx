## The Story

I used to watch sales reps spend four hours writing "personalized" cold emails. They'd open a spreadsheet of fifty leads, open a template, and start hand-editing — first name here, company name there, one sentence pretending they'd read the prospect's LinkedIn. Half the time they'd paste the wrong company into the wrong email and not notice until a prospect wrote back "who is Acme Corp?"

The industry-wide response rate on batch-blasted templates is under one percent. Meaning: forty-nine of the fifty prospects deleted the email in three seconds. Meaning: four hours of a sales rep's day, gone, for zero pipeline. And hiring an SDR to do this at scale costs three to five thousand a month — for work the human is barely paying attention to by lead thirty.

## What I Built

I built an outreach worker that takes a batch of leads and writes a genuinely unique email for each one. Not template-with-first-name unique — actually unique. Subject line tailored to the prospect's role. Opening line that references their specific company, product, or challenge. Under a hundred and twenty words. One clear CTA. All in the sender's tone, not a generic AI voice.

Every email either lands as a Gmail draft the sender can skim before hitting send, or gets sent directly — the team picks the mode. Every generated message gets logged to a sheet so nothing gets sent twice and nobody gets forgotten. And when the batch is done, Slack pings the team with a count.

## The Outcome

The first test batch moved reply rates from around 0.8 percent to 8.4 percent. Fifty leads that used to take four hours now take about ninety seconds. Cost per email is a fraction of a cent. And the copy-paste mistakes that used to embarrass the team — wrong name, wrong company, wrong pitch — disappeared entirely, because there's no copy-paste left in the process.
