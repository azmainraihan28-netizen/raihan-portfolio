## The Story

Every founder I talked to had the same nervous browser tab habit. Once a week, sometimes once a day, they'd manually visit their competitors' pricing pages just to check. Did anyone drop their price? Add a new tier? Change the free-trial length? The reality is that competitors change pricing quietly all the time, and most teams find out because a prospect casually mentions it on a sales call two months later.

By then the founder is already losing deals to a discount they didn't know existed. Pricing intelligence tools that solve this — Prisync, Competera — start north of two hundred a month and are built for enterprise, which is nonsense for a lean SaaS or e-commerce brand.

## What I Built

I built them a quiet weekday watcher. Every business morning it visits the competitor pricing pages they care about, pulls the current pricing and plan structure, compares against yesterday, and if anything changed — a new tier, a price bump, a discount, a removed feature — the team gets a Slack ping with the diff spelled out plainly. Everything gets logged into a sheet, so over time there's a full timeline of the market's pricing moves that the founder can point at when the board asks "what are competitors doing?"

No manual checking. No missed announcements. The founder finds out the same day.

## The Outcome

Weekly manual pricing checks — usually thirty to sixty minutes across a founder plus a product person — collapsed to zero. Cost per scan is under a cent. Prospects stopped surprising the sales team with pricing news from competitors. And the historical pricing log turned into an unexpected strategic asset — the team could actually see patterns in how the market was moving.
