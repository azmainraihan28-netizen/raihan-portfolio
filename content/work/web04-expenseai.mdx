## The Story

ExpenseAI's founders had prototyped a Gmail-based expense sweeper in a weekend. They needed a mobile app that could turn that hack into a real product — with AI receipt scanning, spending intelligence, and a design that felt bank-grade.

## What We Built

A dark, spacious React Native app with Gemini-powered receipt OCR, a Capital Ledger with real-time balance and category donuts, an Intelligence & Audits screen that flags subscriptions and category spikes, Gmail inbox sweeps, and manual ledger entry with a considered payment-mechanism picker. Sign-in via Google or email with 256-bit encryption.

## The Outcome

The app shipped to TestFlight in six weeks. Beta users are logging expenses 4× more often than the founders' old spreadsheet did, and the "Trigger AI Savings Audit" surface is the most-used screen after the ledger.
