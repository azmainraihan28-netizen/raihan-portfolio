## The Story

Trattoria Abruzzesa has been serving handmade pasta and wood-fired pizzas in Rosario for years, but their old site was a photo grid and a phone number. The family wanted the online room to feel as considered as the dining room - same heritage, same warmth, same discipline.

## What We Built

An editorial Next.js site anchored by a serif-driven hero, a "Handmade in Rosario" story block, and a full **Imperial Menu** page with search, dietary filters (vegetarian, gluten-free), and category chips for pasta, pizza, risotto, seafood, desserts, wines, cocktails. Reservations flow through a single "Reserve a Table" button that opens a phone-first form. A Michelin Guide callout and starred quote sit above the fold. Content lives in Sanity so the owners can update dishes and prices without touching code.

## The Outcome

Web reservations doubled in the first two months, and the average party size increased once the menu was legible online instead of hidden behind a PDF. The site now converts as their strongest single acquisition channel outside of walk-ins.
