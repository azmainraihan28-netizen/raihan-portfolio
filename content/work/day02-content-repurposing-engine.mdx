## The Story

Creators kept telling me the same story. They'd spend a full day writing one great blog post or newsletter, hit publish, and then sit down to "make it into social content" - a LinkedIn version, a Twitter thread, an Instagram caption, a hook for a Reel. Two more hours gone. By the time it was all done, the idea was cold and they were exhausted.

The worst part wasn't the time. It was the tax it put on the next piece. Half the reason people ghost their own content calendar is that they know every long-form piece secretly costs a whole second day of repackaging.

## What I Built

I built a repurposing engine that takes one long piece of writing and turns it into six ready-to-post assets in one shot. Drop in a blog post, a transcript, or a newsletter - out comes a LinkedIn version with a scroll-stopping hook, a Twitter thread broken into scannable beats, an Instagram caption, a short-form video hook, an email teaser, and a Reddit-ready reframing. Each one written in the creator's voice, not a generic AI voice. Each one tuned to how that platform actually rewards content.

The creator writes once. The engine handles the ten different tones the internet demands.

## The Outcome

What used to take a full afternoon now takes about ninety seconds. Cost per full fanout is under a cent. Creators who couldn't keep up with even one platform are suddenly present on five. And the long-form pieces they used to dread writing feel worth it again, because a single post now feeds a full week of distribution.
