## The Story

Every Shopify store owner I talked to had the same haunted number in their dashboard - abandoned checkout rate somewhere around seventy percent. Real customers, right at the finish line, walking away with a full cart. The store loses the sale. The ad spend that got them there evaporates. And the "recovery email" the store sends an hour later is the same generic "you left something behind!" everyone in the world has already ignored ten times this week.

The apps that promise to fix this - Klaviyo, Omnisend - start at fifty a month and get expensive fast. And even then, the emails they send are still templates. They don't know *why* the shopper hesitated.

## What I Built

I built them a recovery worker that actually thinks about each abandoned cart. The moment a shopper walks away, the workflow reads the specific cart - what was in it, price point, category, customer history if there is one - and writes a follow-up email tailored to that shopper's likely reason for hesitating. High cart value? The email leans into social proof and a limited-time nudge. Frequent browser who never buys? The email opens with a personal question, not a discount. Skincare cart? The tone matches the brand's voice, not a Shopify template.

The store owner never touches it. The customer gets an email that sounds like a person wrote it, sent from the store, within minutes of leaving.

## The Outcome

Cart recovery rates on the first pilot went from single digits to the mid-teens. Cost per abandoned cart processed is under a cent - meaning the workflow is effectively free at any store volume. Klaviyo-tier email costs got replaced by a spreadsheet log and a Slack ping when a big cart recovers. And the founders finally stopped watching the "abandoned" number climb every hour without a plan.
