## The Story

Every founder I know opens their inbox in the morning and feels the same drop in their chest. Two hundred emails. Most of them are noise - newsletters, receipts, sales pitches, cold outreach. But buried in there are three that actually matter. A big client asking a question. A partnership offer. A support fire waiting for someone to notice.

They spend the first hour of their day just sorting. By the time they answer the important three, they're already tired, and the real work of the day hasn't started. And the worst version of this - the emails they'd want to reply to warmly and thoughtfully - get one-line answers because there's no energy left.

## What I Built

I built an inbox layer that reads every incoming email the moment it lands and does the sorting for them. Cold pitch? Archived. Newsletter? Filed. Real customer question? Flagged as important, and a draft reply is already sitting there - written in the founder's tone, referencing the actual thread, ready to send with one click. Same thing for partnership emails, support tickets, and anything that looks like a real conversation.

The founder opens Gmail and instead of two hundred unread, they see fifteen. Each one already has a draft next to it. All they do is skim, tweak, send.

## The Outcome

Ninety-minute morning triage turned into fifteen minutes. Response times to important emails dropped from same-day to same-hour. Cost of running it is a fraction of a cent per email. And the strange side effect - the one every founder mentioned - was that they started answering more thoughtfully, because the AI handled the tone and they got to focus on the substance.
